# Partner-outreach email — Track 2 (Care Rounds, for home-care agencies)

Send this week — letters have the longest lead time, and an agency contact is a
two-birds partner (real workforce feedback **and** the named letter/pilot
commitment). Aim for a **local, owner-operated agency** where you'll reach a
decision-maker, not a franchise's national line.

**Send to (prioritized — SC Lowcountry starting points):**
1. **A local, owner-operated home-care agency** — e.g. A Better Choice Home Care
   (Charleston/Lowcountry), Home Care Plus (Mt. Pleasant), Nightingale's Nursing
   & Attendants (Charleston), SYNERGY HomeCare of Lowcountry (Hilton Head).
2. A **direct-care workforce organization** or home-care association.
3. Your local **Area Agency on Aging** (find via eldercare.acl.gov).

---

**Subject:** Letter of support — HHS Caregiver AI Prize (Care Rounds, a tool for
home-care aides)

> Dear [FOUNDER: recipient name / agency],
>
> I'm [FOUNDER: your name], a U.S. Air Force veteran and the developer of
> **Care Rounds** — a mobile app built to give home-care **aides** back the time
> they lose to paperwork. The core idea is simple: an aide **talks through a
> visit** and the app writes the structured note for them to review, instead of
> typing it from memory at the end of a shift. It also gives each worker a coach
> grounded in the specific client's real care information, a simple way to flag
> something for a supervisor, and early-warning cues (like a medication running
> low) — all organized around a real day of visits across multiple clients.
>
> Care Rounds is entering **Phase 1 of the HHS / Administration for Community
> Living "Caregiver AI Prize Challenge"** (Track 2 — *Extending the Caregiver
> Workforce*), due July 31, 2026. The competition explicitly weighs **input from
> the workforce it serves and partnerships with the organizations that employ
> them**, and I'd be honored to have your agency involved in any of these ways:
>
> 1. **A brief letter of support** (½ page) for the application appendix — or, even
>    better, a short **letter of interest in piloting** it with a few of your
>    aides in Phase 2.
> 2. **A short feedback conversation** — 15–20 minutes with you and/or a couple of
>    your aides about the day-to-day paperwork and coordination burden, so I'm
>    building *with* the workforce, not guessing.
> 3. **An informal advisory role** as I refine Care Rounds through the competition.
>
> Given the July 31 deadline, anything you can offer by **[FOUNDER: ~1 week out]**
> would be a real help. I'm happy to send a one-page overview or give a quick demo
> at your convenience — it's a working app today.
>
> Thank you for the work your team does. The whole reason I built this is to make
> their day a little lighter.
>
> Warmly,
> [FOUNDER: your name] · [FOUNDER: email] · [FOUNDER: phone] · Juno Code Studio (JCSV One LLC)

**Note on evidence + consent.** If they agree to a feedback conversation, capture
one or two concrete "you said X → I changed / will change Y" notes for Section 1's
User-Centered evidence, and ask permission to reference them (by role is fine).
An agency that both gives feedback **and** signs a letter covers the two
people-dependent criteria at once.
