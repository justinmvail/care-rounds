# Demo video script — Care Rounds (Track 2)

> A ~2.5–3 minute screen-recording walkthrough you narrate. Record against the
> **seeded build** already on the phone (`AUTH=demo SEED=1`) — it has three
> clients, a signed-in worker, a day of cross-client visits, per-client meds with
> real refill runways, and open supervisor flags. Keep it calm and concrete; let
> the product do the talking. `[VO]` = voiceover; **on-screen** = what to do.

## Cold open (0:00–0:15) — the problem, in one line
**On-screen:** Home screen, "Today's visits" card visible.
[VO] "A home-care aide doesn't see one client a day — they run a route of
several. And a big part of every visit isn't care. It's paperwork. Care Rounds
is built to give that time back."

## 1. The day leads with the route (0:15–0:40)
**On-screen:** Point to **Today's visits** — five visits across three clients, one
marked **Now** with a **Start visit** button, others ahead.
[VO] "Home opens on the worker's actual day — a route across their clients, with
the current visit highlighted. One tap on *Start visit*…"
**On-screen:** Tap **Start visit** on the current visit → the app switches to that
client and opens their schedule. Point to the **client bar** at the top.
[VO] "…and the whole app re-centers on that client. That bar at the top always
tells the worker exactly who they're with."

## 2. Switch clients — the data is really theirs (0:40–1:00)
**On-screen:** Tap the **client bar** → switcher sheet → pick **Frank Albright**.
[VO] "Switch to another client and everything follows — Frank's medications, his
routine, his history. Each client is genuinely their own."
**On-screen:** Land on Home for Frank; point to the **"Watch for"** card (refill /
falls signals).
[VO] "And Care Rounds quietly watches for rising need — here, a medication running
low and a recent pattern of falls — each with the plain reason it flagged. No
black box, just the data the worker already entered."

## 3. The flagship — the note that writes itself (1:00–1:55)
**On-screen:** Care tab → **Document visit**. Tap the mic and **speak** naturally,
e.g.: *"Morning visit with Frank. He was a little unsteady getting up but ate
most of breakfast. I helped him with a shower and gave his 8am meds. He mentioned
his knee was sore."*
[VO] "This is the core of it. Instead of typing a note from memory at the end of a
shift, the worker just talks."
**On-screen:** Tap **Write the note** → the structured note appears (summary,
observations, tasks done, anything to flag). Scroll it.
[VO] "The AI turns that into a clean, structured note — and nothing is saved until
the worker reviews it. The human is always the last step."
**On-screen:** If a *needs-attention* banner shows, point to it, then **Save**.
[VO] "If it catches something worth escalating, it flags it — and that flag goes
straight to a supervisor."

## 4. The human escalation channel (1:55–2:20)
**On-screen:** Care → **Team → Flags**. Show the open flags (Frank's near-fall,
Evelyn's refused meds). Tap **Resolve** on one.
[VO] "Flags land in one place for a supervisor. A flag is a task *for a person* —
it stays open until a human closes it. The AI never decides someone's care."

## 5. The coach + My Rounds (2:20–2:45)
**On-screen:** Chat tab → ask the coach a grounded question (e.g. "what should I
keep an eye on with Frank?"). Show it answering from Frank's data.
[VO] "There's a coach for the moment — grounded in this client's real situation,
educational, never diagnostic."
**On-screen:** Rounds tab → the full cross-client day.
[VO] "And *My Rounds* is the worker's whole day across every client."

## Close (2:45–3:00) — the thesis + the ecosystem
**On-screen:** Back to Home.
[VO] "Care Rounds returns time to the people the whole system depends on — and it
shares a backbone with Holdclose, our family-caregiver app, so one day the family
and the paid worker can see the same care picture. Two sides of one care team."

---

### Recording notes
- Turn on **Auto-Lock → Never** and use screen recording; keep taps deliberate.
- If a live coach reply is slow or the device has no shim, pre-load the chat
  thread or narrate over the loading state — don't let dead air undercut it.
- Keep total length **under ~3 minutes**; judges skim.
- Do **not** show the model/vendor name anywhere (it isn't in the UI — keep it
  that way in narration too: "the AI," not a brand).
